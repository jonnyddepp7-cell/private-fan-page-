Clear rules, fair promotions

Giveaway rules preview

Before a promotion opens, publish the organizer’s name, exact prize and value, opening and closing times with time zone, eligible locations and ages, entry methods, entry limits, selection process, winner notification method, prize delivery details, privacy handling and any required legal disclosures.

Do not describe a promotion as live until the prize is secured. Check local laws before running a paid-entry or chance-based promotion. Where applicable, provide a lawful free-entry route and do not make VIP payment a condition of winning unless legally reviewed.

Giveaway rules

Each real giveaway needs its own complete terms. This demo does not operate a live prize draw. Winners should never be asked to pay a release fee or provide passwords, bank PINs or one-time security codes.

Privacy notice preview

Before collecting real data, publish a full privacy notice identifying the operator, data collected, purposes, legal basis where applicable, processors, retention period, user rights, security measures and contact details. This static demo does not submit form data to a server.
